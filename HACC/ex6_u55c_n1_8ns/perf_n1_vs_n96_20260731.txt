Analysis:
- Cycle count: n1 needs ~2.0x the cycles of n96 — NOT ~96x, because this tiny
  workload is dominated by AXI reads/control, not FP compute (each of n96's
  PEs does only 18 MACs; the shared-multiplier serialization in n1 adds ~21.6K
  cycles ≈ 1728 MACs x ~12 cycles/MAC of shared-FPU scheduling).
- Wall time: n1 is only 1.30x slower — its smaller netlist routes better
  (WNS -3.25 vs -6.83) so XRT clocks it 1.54x faster, masking the cycle gap.
- Implication for FactorFlow calibration: at these dims the accelerator is
  memory/control-bound; a compute-bound comparison (and bandwidth data)
  requires the planned larger CONV workload.
